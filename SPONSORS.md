# Sponsors & Funding

**English** · [简体中文](SPONSORS.zh-CN.md)

### Why sponsor?

OpenBuddy is MIT-licensed and free to use, but building a desktop AI workspace at this scale requires:

- 💻 Engineering time (≈ 4 maintainers × 40 hr/week)
- 💸 Infrastructure (CI runners, signing certificates, notarization)
- 🎨 Design and UX research
- 🌐 Translation coordination
- 📚 Documentation writing
- 🧪 Eval infrastructure (LLM API spend)

Sponsor money goes directly to:

1. **Maintainer stipends** — pay the people doing the work.
2. **Infrastructure** — CI, signing, CDN.
3. **Bounties** — funded GitHub Issues for community contributors.
4. **Events** — OpenBuddyCon, regional meetups.
5. **Security audits** — annual third-party review.

### Sponsorship FAQ

#### Is OpenBuddy VC-funded?

No. OpenBuddy is independently funded by sponsors and community contributions. We do not take VC money to preserve independence.

#### Will sponsorship influence the roadmap?

Sponsors get **transparency** (private roadmap calls, early access to designs), but **not veto power**. All technical decisions remain with the maintainer team per [`GOVERNANCE.md`](GOVERNANCE.md).

#### Can my company pay for support?

Yes — see [`SUPPORT.md`](SUPPORT.md) for paid support options. Sponsorship and support contracts are independent.

#### Is sponsorship tax-deductible?

Depends on your jurisdiction. OpenBuddy is in the process of becoming a 501(c)(3) fiscal-hosted project via Open Collective. Contact `finance@openbuddy.dev` for a donation receipt.

#### Can I sponsor in cryptocurrency?

Yes — ETH, BTC, USDC. Email `crypto@openbuddy.dev` for wallet addresses.

#### Can I donate hardware or services?

Yes — we accept:

- Apple Developer account seats (for code signing)
- GitHub Pro / Organization seats
- JetBrains / Visual Studio licenses for contributors
- Cloud credits (AWS, GCP, Azure)

Email `in-kind@openbuddy.dev`.

### Transparency

- All donations are publicly logged in our [Open Collective page](https://opencollective.com/openbuddy).
- Quarterly financial reports posted to [`GOVERNANCE.md`](GOVERNANCE.md).
- Annual 990-equivalent filing for US donors.
